Data Deletion Instructions

App: Threads Hunter · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Threads Hunter stores limited information about public Threads posts it has processed (post ID, text, author username, link and time), plus any reply it published. Records are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask for your data to be deleted sooner at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your Threads username. If you also want our reply removed, include the link to your post.

Operator: [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME]

Email: [YOUR EMAIL]

What happens next

  • Within 30 days we delete every stored record associated with your username.
  • If requested, we delete the reply this app posted under your Threads post.
  • We confirm by email once the deletion is complete.

You can also hide or remove our reply yourself at any time using the standard controls on Threads.
