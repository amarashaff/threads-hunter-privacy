Privacy Policy

App: Threads Hunter · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Threads Hunter is a private tool used by one independent creator (the "operator", "we") who sells their own printable educational worksheets for children. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the operator. This policy explains what information the app processes when it interacts with the Threads platform, and how anyone can ask for their data to be deleted.

1. What the app does

Using the official Threads API from Meta, the app searches for recent public Threads posts on topics such as children's learning activities, handwriting and reading. An AI model drafts a short, helpful reply. Replies are posted from the operator's own Threads account, either after the operator approves them or, for a small number of high-confidence replies without links, automatically. The app posts at most 10 replies per day and never more than one reply per post. The operator can delete any reply.

2. Information we process

  • Public post data from Threads search results: the post ID, text, author username, permalink, timestamp, and the public reply status returned by the API.
  • Replies we publish: the text of our reply and its Threads ID.
  • Operator account credentials: the operator's own Threads access token, which is needed to call the API.

We do not collect private messages, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, follower lists, payment information or any non-public data. We do not use cookies or trackers. We do not build profiles of individuals.

3. How the information is used

  • To decide whether a public post is relevant and appropriate to reply to.
  • To draft and publish a reply, and to avoid replying to the same post or person repeatedly.
  • To show the operator a preview of each candidate reply in a private chat so it can be approved, edited, skipped or deleted.

Information is never sold, rented, or used for advertising targeting, and is never shown to anyone other than the operator.

4. Service providers

To perform the steps above, limited data is passed to these processors:

  • Meta (Threads API), to search public posts and publish replies.
  • An AI language model provider (Google Gemini API or Anthropic Claude API), which receives the text and username of a public post in order to draft a reply.
  • Telegram, used as the operator's private notification channel, which receives the post excerpt, username and draft reply.
  • A cloud server (Amazon Web Services), where the app and its database run.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private database on the operator's server, which only the operator can access. Records of posts and replies are automatically deleted after 90 days. The access token is kept only for as long as the app is in use.

6. Data deletion instructions

If one of your public Threads posts was processed by this app and you want all related data removed, send a request to the contact address below with your Threads username. Within 30 days we will:

  • delete every stored record associated with your username (post text, metadata, and history), and
  • on request, delete any reply this app posted under your Threads post.

We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete. Step-by-step instructions are also on the Data Deletion page. You can also hide or remove our reply yourself at any time using the standard controls on Threads.

7. Children

The app replies to adults (parents, caregivers and teachers) who post publicly. It does not knowingly process information from children. If you believe a post by a child was processed, contact us and it will be deleted.

8. Security

The server is accessible only via key-based SSH. API credentials are stored in restricted files that are not shared or published. Data in transit to the APIs above uses HTTPS.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.
